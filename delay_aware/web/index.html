The evidence behind your options

Flight listings and quoted fares come from SerpApi’s Google Flights results. Airport information comes from OurAirports. Date-specific weather comes from the National Weather Service.

FlightAware AeroAPI supplies available selected-departure identity, recent measured gate-arrival history, and current estimates. Current estimates and historical delay rates are shown separately. Flight history combines BTS Marketing Carrier and Reporting Carrier records, with a target of 24 months. Marketing records identify historical operating flights. Matching uses date, operating airline and flight number, route, and scheduled departure time. Each operation is counted once, source coverage is shown, and conflicting records are excluded. Historical relationships do not verify the operator of a future departure; an exact FlightAware match can establish the selected operating flight separately. History is not a live forecast.
